About this prototype
Why I built this
The Problem
Define and implement engineering processes, tools, and standards that improve POS and Kiosk platform reliability, data integration, overall development velocity, and adoption of the tech stack across 100+ markets. Scale our engineering capabilities while fostering a culture of craftsmanship, collaboration, and continuous improvement across all layers of our restaurant stack.
1
Measure
Before you can improve reliability, velocity, or adoption — you need a baseline. This dashboard defines a composite Platform Readiness Score across four dimensions (reliability, digital adoption, stack currency, feature enablement) so every market has a number that can go up or down.
2
Track progress
Each initiative card shows enrollment, projected score impact, and target date. Over time, the score moves — and you can point to exactly which program moved it.
I've built a similar dashboard at my current company
It's become one of the most useful tools I have for three kinds of conversations:
Manage up In executive quarterly reviews — showing where we are, what we're investing in, and whether it's working.
Manage down Every week with my managers — we walk the metrics together to make sure nothing is drifting.
Manage across In weekly conversations with Product, Operations, and other stakeholders — shared numbers replace subjective status updates.
